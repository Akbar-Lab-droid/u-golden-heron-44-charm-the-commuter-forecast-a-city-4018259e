Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e) bounty **Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't**.

The entry is one line of weather forecast, at most 140 characters, for a city that does not exist: name the place, give it weather, and make a bored commuter smile on the way to work.

## The entry

> Good morning, Vessport: light drizzle of confetti, a 90% chance of applause, and winds gossiping at 12 miles per hour.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` copies the committed `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `out/entry.txt`). Standard library only, no network.
